The Project

A web-based platform built to run the election for leader student representative at school — letting students view candidates and cast their vote online instead of on paper.

The Problem

The paper-based voting system was slow, prone to errors, and took too much time to count manually.

The Solution

A secure web app that lets students vote online from any device. It ensures one vote per student and counts the results instantly.

My Role

I worked as a Full-Stack Developer.

Technology
